# v0.2.0 - English offline edition

Download **codenames-pictures-offline.zip** from the release assets. Extract it completely,
then double-click **LAUNCH_GAME.html**. Keep the **game_assets/** folder beside the launcher.
No installation, server, or internet connection is needed to play.

## Included

- English interface, instructions, and notices.
- Two, three, or four teams, nine presets, and custom rules.
- Drag-to-reorder team cards before dealing.
- Replayable seeds, inline seed switching, and fixed clipboard handling.
- A separate captain key, configurable timers, rankings, and end-game celebration.
- 280 local picture files, with no remote image fallback.
- Credits and copyright information in game_assets/COPYRIGHT.txt.
